# Phase 2 acceptance

The sibling checks for `sim_segments` are printed by `uv run association sim build` (`segments.check_segments`): per season, the share of game seconds in resolved segments, the first-touch five against the box five in period 1, and - over fully resolved games - player-game minutes within one of the box and plus-minus equal to it, with phantoms, one-id and inconsistent substitutions and touches by ids outside the deduped box roster. `results/` holds each recorded run. `plus_minus_conventions.py` is the experiment that chose the same-clock convention (2025: substitutions applied as served matched the box plus-minus in 42.16% of player-games, every substitution at a clock applied after that clock's plays 98.14%, the hybrids 98.10-98.50%); the walk now applies a substitution at once for presence and defers the segment close to the next clock reading, which keeps resolution at 99.9% and matches 97.9-98.6% in 2021-2026. Recorded 2026-10-11: the whole build (plays and segments, 2007-2026) in 44 seconds, a 963 MB `sim.duckdb`.
