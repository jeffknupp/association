# Phase 3 acceptance

The sibling checks for `sim_possessions` and `sim_actions` are printed by `uv run association sim build` (`possessions.check_possessions`): per season, the games kept and excluded (a game whose plays do not sum to the final for both teams), possessions a team-game, the share of team-games whose possession points plus technical free throws equal the final (the hard gate), the mean absolute and signed difference between a team's possessions and the box formula `FGA - OREB + TOV + 0.44 FTA` over its player lines, the share of games where the two teams' counts differ by at most the number of periods, and the exact-match share of player-games for FGA, FTA, OREB, DREB, AST and TOV summed from actions against the box, with the walk's own counts (and-ones, lone one-of-ones, implied changes, jump-ball changes, offensive fouls, rebound-side disagreements, rebounds with no miss pending, possessions dropped for having neither action nor time).

`build_and_check.py NBA_DUCKDB SIM_DUCKDB` builds all three phases into `SIM_DUCKDB` and prints that table plus the facts behind `possessions.py`'s docstring: possessions a team-game three ways (all games, regulation-only, per 48 team-minutes), the end- and start-type mix, the shape of the implied changes, actions per team-game by kind, and the timing. `results/` holds each recorded run, named by date, against this checkout's `nba.duckdb` (copied 2026-09-24). Recorded 2026-10-12: see `results/build_2026-10-12.out` (the whole build, 2007-2026).

The rules the data forced, each measured on 2025 before being written (the queries are one-off reads of `sim_plays` recorded in the plan's section 0b, 0.14): a lone `1 of 1` keeps the ball for the fouled team; a flagrant or clear-path trip keeps the ball; a jump ball names the gainer but ends nothing itself, because ESPN logs a turnover-and-steal next in two of three held balls the defense wins; the jump ball's gainer is the third participant and his team comes from the roster, since ESPN's `team_id` on a jump ball is a coin flip in 2013-2017.
